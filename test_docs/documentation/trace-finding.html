How to find any trace — whether it's active on the board or archived months ago — and how to jump straight to one by its number.

New to Trace? See Trace — Overview & how to use it first.

Two ways to look: Board and List

The toolbar has a Board / List toggle.

  • Board — the kanban of cards, for working the active queue day to day.
  • List — a dense, sortable table. Better for scanning a lot of traces at once, and the home of the archive.

Filtering on the board

Above the board you can narrow what's shown by location, assignee (including "assigned to me"), priority, type, and overdue, plus a free-text search on the title. Clearing the filters brings everything back.

The List view & the archive

Switch to List for the table. At the top, a selector chooses which set of traces to show:

  • Active — the same traces as the board.
  • Resolved — recently resolved traces.
  • Closed — the archive: everything that has been closed.
  • All — everything, regardless of status.

For the archived sets you can add a date range — so you can answer questions like "what did we resolve at this shop last month?" The same filters as the board (location, assignee, priority, type) apply here too, and you can sort by clicking the column headers.

This is how you find a closed trace again. Once a trace is archived it leaves the board — but it's never gone. List → Closed (with a date range and a filter or two) brings it right back.

Jumping straight to a trace

Every trace has a unique #number — shown on its card and at the top of its detail view. It's the simplest way to refer to a trace: just say "have a look at Trace 312".

To open one by number, type it into the "Go to #" box in the toolbar and press Enter. The trace opens immediately. If the number doesn't exist — or you don't have access to it — you'll see "Not found".

The number is the reference. Rather than copying links around, lean on the #number — it's short, it never changes, and anyone on the team can open it with Go to # from wherever they are.

In short

  • Board for active work; List for scanning and the archive.
  • Filter by location, assignee, priority, type, overdue, and text — everywhere.
  • List → Closed + a date range finds anything that's been archived.
  • Go to # opens any trace by its number — the easiest way to point someone at one.
