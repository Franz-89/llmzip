4. Prizes were redeemed (cascos, altavoz maletin — 6,900 tickets spent just before the final consolidation)

7. After migration to Sapiens, the card continued to be used for free plays and prize redemptions

**The name "Mary" in the reset comment matches Jimenez, Mary Angeles** — a staff member who performed multiple consolidation operations on this card chain in Sacoa.
