Conclusion & Recommendations

Card #616952 • Updated 27/03/2026 • Card 971089 analysis included

Assessment

Anomalous consolidation pattern spanning 3+ years (Jan 2022 – Mar 2026) and two systems (Sacoa → Sapiens).

Card 971089 received 25+ consolidations from different source cards over 2 years — far above any normal card usage. The entire balance was then funnelled through a linear chain (971089 → 1649808 → 1717787) into Sapiens, where it was used for free games and prize redemptions. 7 staff members were involved across the chain.

Whether the consolidations in Sacoa started as legitimate operations (e.g., cleaning up abandoned cards) cannot be fully determined without examining the source cards. However, the behaviour in Sapiens removes much of the doubt: on 5 separate occasions, someone brought the card to the counter exclusively to collect physical prizes without playing a single game — not even one attempt. This is not normal customer behaviour. It is consistent with someone who knows the card has value and is systematically extracting it.

Key Findings

  • 25+ cards consolidated into card 971089 — this is the core anomaly, far above any normal card usage pattern
  • All consolidations flow through one linear chain: source cards → 971089 → 1649808 → 1717787. The source cards at each stage are different
  • The chain traces back to card 1239432 — 174.9 EUR bonus transferred by Gornostay Olha in Jan 2022. Card 1239432's history is unknown
  • Only ~44 EUR of real money loaded over 2 years on card 971089 — combined with "Pruebas de app" admin recharges, it may have been a service/test card
  • Recurring gaps (4–6 months) in card usage across both Sacoa and Sapiens, consistent with someone who visits the venue occasionally
  • Prize-only visits in Sapiens (verified from transaction log): on 5 out of 8 prize redemption dates, no games were played — someone brought the card to collect prizes without playing (Sep 9, Oct 23, Oct 28, 2025; Jan 31, Mar 8, 2026)
  • Physical prizes redeemed at every stage: ~7,700 tickets from 971089, ~6,900 from 1649808, ~7,775 from the Sapiens card
